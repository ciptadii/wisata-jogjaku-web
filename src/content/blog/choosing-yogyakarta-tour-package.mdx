## Start with the starting prices and their limits

The available starting prices are:

- **1-day package:** from **IDR 550k**.
- **2-day package:** from **IDR 1.1m**.
- **3-day package:** from **IDR 1.65m**.

These are starting prices for the selected package, not per-person prices. Admission tickets and parking are excluded from all three starting prices. Prices may also change during holiday periods. Confirm the applicable price for your dates before making a decision.

Starting prices help frame a budget, but they do not show how well a route fits your needs. Two plans of the same duration can feel very different once distances between sights, available time, and the group’s pace are considered.

## When a 1-day package makes sense

A one-day option suits travellers with very limited time or those who want to focus on one part of Yogyakarta. It works best when priorities are clear and the chosen sights follow a reasonably aligned route.

A single day can be used to enjoy several key experiences without trying to represent the entire city. If arrival or departure takes place on that same day, reduce your expectations for the number of stops further. Reaching the accommodation, handling luggage, and travelling from the starting point still need to be considered.

The central question is not how many places can be included, but what experience you want to take away. One area enjoyed properly can be more valuable than a cross-city route that leaves only brief visits at each stop.

## When to choose a 2-day package

Two days provide room to separate two travel themes. For planning purposes, for example, one day could focus on city experiences and another on an area beyond the centre. The actual division should still reflect your chosen locations rather than simply following a generic list.

This duration suits groups seeking variety without filling every day with long transfers. It offers more opportunity to place meal breaks, shopping time, or a slower visit into the plan. Optional choices for the second day can also be adjusted after seeing how the first day feels.

However, two days do not automatically make every combination practical. If the list covers distant areas, selection is still necessary. The added value lies in a more sensible division of routes, not in doubling the list of sights.

## When a 3-day package is a better fit

Three days are relevant for travellers who want to combine several interests, explore more than one corridor, or leave room for weather and changing travel conditions. This duration also creates the option of placing quieter periods between more active plans.

A three-day plan should still have structure. Set one main focus for each day, then position additional stops according to direction and the group’s energy. Avoid filling open time simply because it exists; unstructured time can be an important part of the journey.

This option is useful when members of the group have different interests. History, food, nature, and shopping can be balanced more comfortably without turning every day into a race through a list. Even so, set priorities so that key experiences are not displaced by additional stops.

## Compare suitability, not duration alone

Before choosing, assess each package through a few practical questions:

- How much sightseeing time is genuinely available outside arrival and departure?
- Can the main sights be grouped without substantial cross-city travel?
- Does the pace suit children, older travellers, or anyone with particular needs?
- Has the budget accounted for admission, parking, meals, and personal spending?
- Does the plan leave room for weather, traffic, and changing preferences?

Request itinerary and service details for the package you are considering. Do not assume facilities or itineraries are identical simply because their durations match. Clarify the pickup point, order of sights, scope for route adjustments, and any other costs you should prepare for.

## Confirm before deciding

Prepare your travel dates, number of participants, accommodation area, main interests, and arrival or departure schedule before asking questions. This information makes the discussion specific from the outset.

Use the **Contact** menu in the site interface to request package details, confirm current prices, and ask about changes during holiday periods. The best choice is the duration that fits your actual time, priorities, and group comfort, rather than simply the longest list or the lowest starting price.
